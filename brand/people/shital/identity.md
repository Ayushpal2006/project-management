## Must match (the face checklist)

Check every published face against `grid/middle-centre.jpg` (DSC00173) and this list.

- **Face shape:** soft oval, close to round. Full cheeks, soft jawline, rounded chin. Face is wide at the cheekbones, not angular.
- **Skin tone:** medium wheatish brown with a warm, golden undertone. Never lighten, whiten or "glow" it. Natural texture with a few small dark spots and faint marks on the cheeks and chin, plus light under-eye shadow. Don't airbrush these away.
- **Eyes / brows:** dark brown, almond-shaped, medium size, slightly heavy upper lids. Brows dark, fairly thick and natural, nearly straight with a soft arch at the outer third.
- **Nose / jaw:** medium nose with a soft, rounded, slightly broad tip. Soft jaw, no strong angles.
- **Lips:** medium-full, usually in a muted rose/mauve lipstick. Smile is wide and shows the top row of teeth (DSC00104, DSC00059).
- **Hair and hairline:** long (past the chest), dark brown-black with slightly browner lengths. Centre parting (very slightly off centre), flat and straight at the roots, soft loose waves from the jaw down. Worn open, falling in front of both shoulders. No fringe.
- **Beard / moustache:** n/a.
- **Glasses:** none.
- **Marks (which side):** a small dark mark on the lower jaw near the chin on her right side (viewer's left in a front photo); scattered small marks on both cheeks. Keep them, and keep them on the same side.
- **Jewellery (part of her recognisable look):** small gold hoop earrings; a gold wristwatch on her left wrist (two different watches appear across the shoot); a thin red-orange thread (kalava) on her right wrist; a slim ring on her left hand.
- **Not seen in any photo:** bindi, nose ring, glasses, heavy makeup. Don't add them.
- **Usual expression on camera:** big, expressive "teacher face". Range runs from a warm smile to exaggerated shock, doubt and stress (see Expression library).

## Signature look

- **Outfit:** blush/ivory satin long-sleeve shirt with a deep V camp collar, small self buttons and slit "keyhole" openings on the forearms; high-waisted black trousers. On team slides: the black polo from `Sheetal.png`.
- **Colours she brings:** blush ivory, black, gold, warm wood. These sit naturally on the brand's white ground and beside the pink-to-orange gradient.
- **Lighting that suits her:** soft, bright, frontal key at eye level, slightly warm, very little shadow (set A is near high-key; sets C and D are warmer). Keep that soft frontal light in composites; hard side light or cold blue grading makes her look unlike herself.
- **Poses that work:** pointing up into headline space, finger on lips, card or note held up beside the face, hand on chin, both hands on head, finger to temple, open-palm shrug.
- **Avoid:** mirroring her (it moves the parting, the chin mark, the watch and the thread), slimming or reshaping the face, skin lightening, changing her outfit inside one piece, AI-generated "new" angles.

## Brand fit (symmetry and semantics)

- **Semantics.** "Didi" means the caring elder sister who has done it already. Her photos carry the emotion of the hook (shock, secret, doubt), and the type carries the fix. Pair the emotion with the slide's job: problem slides get doubt, stress or stern; reveal slides get shh or surprise; payoff and CTA slides get the warm smile. Use exaggerated faces on hooks only, and give the payoff slide a calm or smiling face so the piece doesn't feel like clickbait.
- **Symmetry.** Her face is fairly balanced, but the parting, the chin mark, the watch (left wrist) and the red thread (right wrist) all give away a mirrored photo. **Never flip her.** If the layout needs her facing the other way, pick a different photo or move the text.
- **Composition (brief §9 and golden ratio).** Layouts are asymmetric and left-aligned, so she usually sits on the right third with the headline in the open space on the left. Put her eyes on the upper phi line (0.382 from the top). Pointing photos (00064-00066, 00157, 00164) should point into the headline. Card shots (00104-00110) let you swap the card for an app icon, UI screenshot or price tag.
- **Cut-outs.** The shoot backgrounds are busy (shelves, figurines, plant). For the white carousel look, cut her out (rembg `isnet-general-use` works; check hair edges and the gaps between arm and body) and place her on white with a soft contact shadow. Keep her own colours; don't recolour the shirt to match the gradient.
- **Colour.** Blush shirt + white ground + pink-to-orange gradient words + black numerals is a clean match. On near-black video frames, add a soft warm rim or keep the original background blurred, because the light shirt glows against black.

## Do / Don't for AI image tools

- **Do** use real photos first. If an AI tool must make a new scene, attach at least 3 real photos (DSC00173 master, DSC00104 smile, DSC00143 turned), and say "keep her exact face, skin tone and hair; change only the background, outfit or lighting I describe".
- **Do** keep the face, skin tone, chin mark, hairline and hoop earrings exactly as in the references.
- **Don't** let the tool lighten her skin, slim her face, sharpen her jaw, enlarge her eyes, add a bindi, nose ring, glasses or heavy makeup, or change her hair to straight, short or curly.
- **Don't** generate profile or 3/4 views she has never been photographed in; ask for a photo instead.
- **Don't** use glossy 3D, plastic skin or studio-stock lighting (brief §9).
