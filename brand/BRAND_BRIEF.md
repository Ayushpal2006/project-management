_Last updated: 2026-10-30. File paths in §2, §6 and §7 are relative to the brand asset folder. In this repo that folder is `brand/assets/` (copy the contents of the local `The Skilled brains/` folder there; it stays out of git while the repo is public). ## 1) Identity

**The Skilled Brains** is an Indian education video brand (Instagram handle seen on our carousels: `@theskilledbrains`). We make short, punchy videos and carousels that teach practical, high-income and AI skills — AI tools, content creation, editing, freelancing and careers.

- **Audience:** Indian students and early-career learners (college students, job seekers, freshers, young working professionals) who want useful skills fast, in a language they actually speak.
- **Language:** Hinglish voiceover / scripts; **English-only on-screen text**.
- **Promise:** one clear, usable takeaway per post, explained simply.
- **Do not cite `skilledbrains.com`** — it belongs to an unrelated consulting firm, "Skilled Brains Inc". We have no connection to it.

## 2) Logo usage

The mark is a rounded, cloud-shaped **brain** drawn with a continuous **"S"** running through it (with small neuron "branches"). It comes as an **icon only** or **icon + stacked wordmark** ("The / Skilled / Brains", heavy rounded geometric sans, left-aligned beside the icon).

> Note: `all logos.jpeg` is a screenshot of a file browser showing eight files named `1.jpg`–`8.jpg`; those originals are **not** in this folder. Ask for them before using any variant other than `Logo.png` in production (the screenshot is low-res and JPEG-compressed).

**Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo of at least the height of the "S" stroke-loop — practically, **≥ 25% of the icon's height on every side**. In carousels, the small icon sits top-left inside its own rounded square (as on the ChatGPT Codes slides).

**Minimum size:** icon ≥ 48 px tall on screen; lockup ≥ 120 px wide (below that, use the icon only).

**Misuse — don't:**
- recolour outside the approved pairs above (e.g. no pink/yellow logo — the motion accents are for type and graphics, not the mark);
- place the mint mark on busy photos without a solid or dark-scrim backing;
- stretch, rotate, outline, add drop-shadows/glows, or re-typeset the wordmark;
- separate the "S" from the brain or rearrange the stacked wordmark into one line;
- put the green-on-green variants on non-green colours.

## 5) Tone of voice

- **Clear** — one idea, said simply. No jargon without an everyday example.
- **Energetic** — fast pace, confident, a little playful.
- **Student-first** — speak like a smart senior/didi/bhaiya helping you, not a lecturer.
- **Hinglish voiceover, English on-screen text only.**
- **Visual discipline:** minimal frames, **one message per frame**, **no split screens, no lower thirds**.
- CTAs are direct and specific (e.g. "Comment \"CODE\"" → resource in DMs).
