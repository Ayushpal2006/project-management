## Three pillars, one face each

Every carousel belongs to one pillar. **The pillar owner's real photo goes on the cover and on any slide that shows a person.**

| Pillar | Owner (cover face) | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Content Creation | **Sheetal** (owns the whole pillar) | Instagram and YouTube growth, hooks, retention, storytelling, content strategy, creator case studies, experiments, our own analytics |
| 2. Video Editing | **Ujjwal** for core / pro editing; **Yash Ranjan Mishra** for vibe edits, trends, AI clipping | Pacing, sound, Premiere Pro, client process; quick edits, OpusClip, phone vs PC |
| 3. Generative AI | **Sheetal** for the Earn Money and Productivity tracks; **Yash Ranjan Mishra** for New Tools | ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini workflows, CVs, job hunting, mock interviews, realistic earning; new tool tests with Keep or Skip verdicts |

When two owners work together (for example Human vs OpusClip, or Ujjwal reviewing Yash's edit), put both faces on the cover. Use DSR (Digraj sir) only for founder or brand-wide posts that fit no single pillar.

## Picking the photo

- Use the real shoot photos, never AI faces (`skilled-brains-face-identity`). Sheetal's shoot is in the Mac folder `The Skilled brains/sheetal didi thumbnail images/` (about 120 photos) and on the team Drive.
- Match the pose to the hook: a finger on the lips for "don't post yet", hands on the head for a mistake, a finger on the temple for "why did this work?", holding a card or note for a freebie CTA.
- The first slide is the strongest visual element and gets the most design care (board: carousel process).
- Keep photos out of git while the repo is public. They go in each design's gitignored `people/` folder.

## How every piece should feel (Creative Formula)

Entertain first → Demonstrate → Teach → Invite action. Avoid generic tutorials and listicles. Recurring formats (inside the pillars, not extra pillars): Human vs AI, Before vs After, Challenges, Experiments, Tool Tests, Creator Breakdowns, Real-World Transformations, Educational Demonstrations, Trend-Based Indian Content, Case Studies.

## Publishing system

- Reels rotate Content Creation → Video Editing → Gen AI → repeat (no fixed weekday per topic).
- One Instagram carousel every day. Themes: AI prompt packs, Free vs Paid AI tools, Google Flow guides, editing cheat sheets, content creation hooks, creator growth breakdowns, practical career AI prompts, festival AI creatives.
- Carousel process: Research → strong first-slide hook → slide content → design → review → publish.
- Courses come after free value and only when relevant: Instagram and YouTube courses (Content Creation); Mobile Editing, Desktop/Premiere Pro, the 3-month Video Editing Cohort (Video Editing); the Gen AI Course and Gen AI Mini Cohort (Gen AI).
