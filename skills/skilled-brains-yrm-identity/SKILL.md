## How to use him

1. Pick the cell from the matrix that the slide needs, use the original photo, and crop so his eyes sit on the 0.382 line.
2. Cover: front, looking into the lens, glasses on, warm smile (#239, #205, #222, #226, #362, #487). Men in carousels = YRM (user rule).
3. Keep layouts asymmetric and left-aligned; put him on the side the text isn't. If he faces the wrong way for the layout, move the text, never flip him.
4. With an AI image tool, attach #239 plus one or two shots in the same angle, and paste the prompt block in `brand/people/yrm/identity.md`. Reject any output whose glasses, beard, hair or skin tone drift.
5. Resemblance check before delivery: put the render next to #239 at the same size. If a teammate would hesitate for a second, redo it.
