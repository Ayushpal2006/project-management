# The Skilled Brains: motion graphics style

The user approved this style on 2026-10-21. Apply it to every edit.

> **Later update (2026-10-29):** the deeper reference study in `skilled-brains-reference-look` drops glow, bloom and light leaks and sets one palette per video from the footage. Where the two differ, follow `skilled-brains-reference-look`. The brand anchors in `skilled-brains-color-type` still apply.

## Hard rules (never break)
1. All on-screen text and supers are in ENGLISH, even when the script/voiceover is Hinglish. Never put Hinglish on screen.
2. NO split screens and NO lower-third text. Graphics and text are FULL SCREEN.
3. Keep frames minimal. One key message dominates the frame. Use few decorative elements, at low opacity, so they never distract from the teacher's point.
4. The hook (first ~60-80s) gets the strongest, richest animation.
5. Deliver EDITABLE project files (Premiere Pro XML/project + After Effects ExtendScript/.aep with fonts bundled), not just a rendered video.

## Look
- Dark, near-black backgrounds with soft glows, bloom and blurred light leaks. High contrast.
- Grain/noise over every gradient for a risograph/retro-print feel. Never flat, clean vector.

## Palette
- Main accents: hot pink, lemon yellow, white.
- Secondary sets: violet + electric blue, and orange + cream.
- Premium/data variant: muted gold, olive green, brown, with elegant serif numbers.

## Elements
- Soft 3D-ish glossy objects: glass bubbles, inflated shapes, keycaps, lightbulbs, smileys.
- Sparkles, 4-point stars, asterisks, starbursts.
- UI language, used sparingly: black pill labels with a white outline (e.g. "LOADING..."), pixel-art hand cursors, selection boxes with corner handles, thin grid lines, orbit rings.

## Typography
- Headlines and kinetic words: Clash Display (geometric sans). Big letters, scattered/rotated, partial blur.
- Body/secondary: Satoshi.
- Numbers and stats: Playfair Display (serif). STIX Two Text for formulas.
- Pill labels: Silkscreen (pixel font).

## Data style
Big serif numbers, candlesticks, pie slices and line charts in warm gold tones.

## Motion
- Pop/scale-ins with overshoot, playful bouncy timing.
- Floating and orbiting objects, light-ray bursts, blur-to-focus reveals.
- Cursors that click things into action.
- Energetic but smooth.

## Reference example (physics boards video)
- Script: "Class 12 Physics: Most Important High Weightage Topics", built on a shopping/discount metaphor. Chapters are tiered low / moderate / max weightage. Example key super: "YDSE: asked 7x since 2015".
- Style frames made for it: hook "discount" frame, "add to cart" frame, and a "YDSE 7 times" stat frame. Each is full screen on a dark background with a single dominant message, pink/yellow glow, grain, a glossy object and a pill label.
- Storyboard: 60 scenes in sections Hook, Budget, Low, Moderate, Max, Course, Outro.

## Workflow notes
- B-roll comes from the user's Google Drive. The Drive connector only downloads files under 10 MB, so ask for trimmed clips or audio-only MP3s (mono, 48 kbps) of raw footage for transcription. The edit XML then links back to the original 4K files.
- Before building anything big, show static style frames first and get approval.
