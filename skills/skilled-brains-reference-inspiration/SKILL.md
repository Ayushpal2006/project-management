# Taking inspiration from references (never copying)

The user will send posts they like while scrolling. Our job is to learn **why** each one works and apply that idea in The Skilled Brains' own look. We never reproduce someone else's post.

## When a reference arrives

1. **Look at it properly.** Open the link or image. For a carousel, look at every slide. For a video, sample the hook (first 5 s), a mid section and the end.
2. **Log it** in `brand/references/INDEX.md` (one row per reference, newest first): date, link, creator, format, what works, what we'll take, what we won't, tags. Save a copy of the image locally in `brand/references/files/` only for study; those files stay out of git.
3. **Extract the principle, not the pixels.** Write what works as a rule that would apply to any topic, for example "the hook slide asks a question the viewer can't answer yet" or "number in a giant serif, explanation in one short line".
4. **Update the brand system** if the reference teaches something lasting:
   - layout or composition idea: the brief's reference section and, if it is a rule, `skilled-brains-golden-ratio`
   - colour or font idea: propose it to the user (colours and fonts are their call), then `skilled-brains-color-type`
   - a face, pose or angle idea for a team member: their `identity.md` ("Poses that work")
   - add a changelog line to `brand/BRAND_BRIEF.md`
5. Tell the user in one or two lines what you took from it.

## Making content from a reference: the originality test

Before delivering anything inspired by a reference, all of these must be true:

- [ ] **Words are ours.** No sentence, hook line or caption copied or lightly reworded.
- [ ] **Colours are ours.** Palette comes from the brief, not from the reference.
- [ ] **Fonts are ours.** From the current pairing, not the reference's.
- [ ] **Faces are ours.** Our team or licensed imagery; no other creator's photos, characters, mascots or logos.
- [ ] **Layout is changed in at least two ways** (element order, proportions, position of face vs text, number of slides, framing).
- [ ] **It passes the side-by-side test:** put ours next to the reference. Someone who follows both accounts should not think ours is a copy or a template swap.

If a request is effectively "make it exactly like this", say so, and offer a version that keeps the idea in our style.

## Older content of our own

Our own past posts are fair to reuse and remix. When the user shares our Instagram or YouTube links, log the best-performing ones in `INDEX.md` with the tag `own` and note what worked (hook, format, length), so new pieces build on it.
