# Ujjwal ke 5 Shorts / Reels scripts (Hinglish)

Draft: 4 Nov 2026. Presenter: **Ujjwal Jain**. Format: YouTube Short, Instagram Reel pe cross-post.
Har script 45 se 55 second ki hai, Ujjwal ki speed (lagbhag 220 words per minute) ke hisaab se.

**Rules jo follow kiye hain:**
- Poora voiceover Hinglish mein hai, Roman script mein, waise hi jaise Ujjwal bolta hai.
- **On-screen text English mein hi rakha hai.** Ye brand ka hard rule hai (`AGENTS.md`): voice Hinglish,
  screen pe text English, ek frame mein ek hi message. Split screen aur lower thirds nahi.
- Har fact Ujjwal ki apni videos se liya hai (source aur timestamp diya hai). Jo confirm nahi ho paaya,
  uspe `{{check}}` laga hai. Record karne se pehle Ujjwal se ye bharwa lena.
- Kuch bhi publish ya schedule nahi hua hai.

## Ujjwal kaise bolta hai (jis base pe likha hai)

Source: [smaltFr5nsw], [696lcETSaE0], [1aGnFhi2FaY], [kcsOP5ZnkqQ].

- Uske words: "bhai", "guys", "honestly", "sach bataun", "matlab", "dekho", "utaawle mat bano bhai",
  "perfect time ka wait mat karo".
