# Vibe Editing: AI Editing vs Human Editing. 20 source clips

Compiled 2026-11-04. Each clip is 50 to 90 seconds, which suits Opus Clip, an AI edit and a human edit.
Timestamps come from each video's YouTube "most replayed" peak (or its chapter), and I checked them by transcribing that window locally with whisper.cpp. View counts are as of today.
Every source is 1M+ views except clips 7 and 8, which are flagged.

**What the series tests.** Opus Clip and AI editors handle captions, reframing, zooms and pace well. A human editor is better at tone, sensitive subjects, comic timing, B-roll choices and story order. Each "Why it's a good test" line says which side the clip should favour.

**Rights.** Only the Social Brains clips belong to the team. For the SRK and Raj Shamani clips, credit the source on screen and keep them short and transformative (a side-by-side comparison with commentary). Don't repost them raw.
