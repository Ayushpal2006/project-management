# Diwali Skill Week: ChatGPT fallback prompts

Six push notification banners, 1024 × 512 px, dated 3–8 November 2026. Use these if you want to regenerate a banner in ChatGPT instead of the cloud render.

**Attach to every prompt:** the Skilled Brains logo (`logo-forest.png` in this folder; `logo-ivory.png` for Nov 8) (forest green on ivory, or white on dark for Nov 8), plus the matching photo plate from this folder (`d03.jpg` … `d08.jpg`) if you want to keep the same scene.

**Shared design system (paste at the top of each prompt):**

> Landscape push notification banner, exactly 1024 × 512 px. Premium editorial education brand. Warm ivory background (#F5EFE4), forest green (#012816) headline text, one key word in festive burnt orange (#C2601A), muted gold (#B8862F) for the small date. Realistic product photography with natural window light on the RIGHT 55% of the frame; the LEFT 45% is calm ivory negative space holding the text. Top-left: the attached logo, then a small letter-spaced label "DIWALI SKILL WEEK" with the date in gold. Bottom-left: a two-line, all-caps, heavy geometric sans headline (like Clash Display Bold), about 10% of the banner height per line, 56 px from the left edge. Subtle 4% film grain. No other text. No glossy 3D, no floating icons, no sparkles or heavy glow, no prices, discounts, offers or testimonials, no people.
