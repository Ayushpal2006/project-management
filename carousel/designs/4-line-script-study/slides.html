The mistake
Most students start like this:

“Good morning everyone, today I’m going to talk about…”

“Hi guys, welcome back to my channel…”

By line 2, people have stopped listening.

Line 1
Hook
First 3 seconds
Start with their problem, not your intro.

“Today I’ll share some study tips.”

“You forget formulas because you only read them.”

Problem
Say what they’re already feeling.

“Studying is very important for exams.”

“Reading feels like studying, but it fades fast.”
