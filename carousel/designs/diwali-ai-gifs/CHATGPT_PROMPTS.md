# Diwali AI GIFs carousel: ChatGPT art prompts

Make 3 images in ChatGPT (image generation). For each one: open a NEW chat, upload the photo named below, paste the prompt, and download the PNG.

**Where to save:** `Project Management /The Skilled brains/Diwali AI GIFs/art/` on the Mac, with the exact filename given. ChatGPT can't save there itself, so download the PNG and drop it into that folder (rename it if needed). I pick it up from there and animate it into the GIF slides myself, so you don't need to make GIFs in ChatGPT.

If a face doesn't look like the real person, reply in the same chat: "The face must match the uploaded photo exactly: same face shape, eyes, nose, smile, hairline, beard and glasses. Redo it." Keep the best of 2-3 tries.

```

LIKENESS (most important): he must be instantly recognisable as the man in the photo. Change his red polo into a simple deep forest-green kurta (#012816). Behind him: a plain warm cream wall (#F6F0E4) with a string of small round warm-yellow fairy lights across the upper back, and 3 lit clay diyas on a ledge at the bottom of the frame. Two or three flat paper lanterns in marigold orange (#F08A24) and gold (#E2AE4A). One small mint-green (#00D579) accent somewhere (a lantern tassel or diya rim).

COMPOSITION: portrait. Keep the TOP 35% of the image almost empty (plain cream wall with only the fairy lights) because a big headline goes there. His head starts around 38% from the top. Do not put anything important in the bottom 8%.

COLOURS: cream background, forest green, marigold orange, gold, warm skin tones, one small mint accent. No pink or purple.

NO text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no border, no frame.

Size: 1024 x 1536 px (2:3 portrait), highest quality.
```
