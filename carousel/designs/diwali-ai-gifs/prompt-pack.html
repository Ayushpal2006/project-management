Sparkler night

The look

Flat grain-art portrait on a cream wall, a lit sparkler in your hand, paper lanterns, fairy lights and three diyas.

Best photo

Front-facing, chest up, smiling at the camera.

Step 1: the picture
Turn the uploaded photo into a flat "grain art" illustration of the SAME person for Diwali. The person must be instantly recognisable: keep the exact face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, smile, hair, skin tone, facial hair and glasses if any. Do not make them slimmer, younger or lighter-skinned. Style: modern flat editorial illustration with a visible risograph grain texture, simple bold shapes, no outlines, no 3D, no anime. Scene: chest-up portrait holding a lit sparkler at shoulder height, wearing a deep green kurta (or a festive outfit of your choice). Plain warm cream wall, a string of small warm fairy lights across the top, 2-3 flat paper lanterns in orange and gold, three lit clay diyas on a ledge at the bottom. No text, no logo, no border. 2:3 portrait.
Step 2: make it move
Animate this exact illustration into a seamless 4-second loop. Keep the art style, colours, grain, framing and face exactly the same. The camera stays still. Only these move: the sparkler fizzes and throws small golden sparks, the diya flames flicker, the fairy lights twinkle, the lanterns sway slightly, and the person gives a small smile and one slow blink. No new objects, no text, no zoom. The last frame must match the first. 4:5 portrait, no audio.
