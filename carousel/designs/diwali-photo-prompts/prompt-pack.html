Prompt Pack

5 prompts that turn your normal photo into a Diwali portrait. Same face, festive look.
01

Open ChatGPT (or Gemini) and upload one clear photo of yourself. Good light, face visible, looking at the camera.

02

Copy one prompt from this pack and paste it in the same message.

03

Send, wait, download. Try the next look with the same photo.

If your face changes: reply in the same chat with "Keep my face exactly like the uploaded photo. Only change the clothes, background and lighting."
Want it sharper? Ask for "4:5 portrait, high detail, natural skin texture".
PROMPT 01 / 05

Diya night

THE LOOK

Ivory silk kurta, rows of lit diyas, marigolds and string lights. The city glow stays behind you.

BEST PHOTO

An evening or night photo, facing the camera.

COPY THIS
Edit this exact photo. Keep the same person, the same pose, the same camera angle, framing and composition. Keep my face exactly as it is: same face shape, skin tone (do not lighten), eyes, brows, hair and facial hair, and keep my glasses or jewellery if I wear them. Do not mirror the photo. Change only: dress me in an ivory silk kurta (or an ivory silk saree or suit) with a thin gold border; turn the background into a Diwali night with rows of lit clay diyas on a ledge, marigold garlands and warm string lights overhead, and soft golden diya light on my face. Keep any city lights softly blurred. Real photo look, natural skin texture, gentle film grain. No text, no logo, no fireworks, no cartoon or 3D. 4:5 portrait.
